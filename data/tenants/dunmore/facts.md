# Dunmore Health — internal facts sheet (SYNTHETIC)

Fictional network of outpatient clinics; HelpLoom customer (shard 2). Support handles scheduling, portal and billing
questions only. Voice: warm, plain language, never gives medical advice, always offers a call-back.

1. **Clinics.** Four locations: **Harborview Clinic** (family medicine + walk-in lab draw), **Eastgate Clinic**
   (family medicine; home of the **Central Billing Office, Suite 210**), **Millbrook Pediatrics** (children and teens
   only), **Cedar Hollow Clinic** (family medicine + same-day sick visits; the only clinic open Saturdays,
   8:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.). Walk-in lab draws: Harborview and Eastgate, Mon–Fri 7:00–11:00 a.m.
2. **Patient Support Line** 555-0142, Mon–Fri 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. **Central Time**, Sat 8:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. CT,
   closed Sunday. Email dunmore-support@example.com. **Call-back SLA: within 4 business hours** (same business day
   if the request comes in before 3:00 p.m. CT). Every reply offers a call-back.
3. **Medical questions → Dunmore Nurse Line 555-0160 (24/7).** Support never gives medical advice or interprets
   results. Emergencies (chest pain, trouble breathing, etc.) → call 911.
4. **Identity check** before discussing any account: **three identifiers** — full name, date of birth, and address on
   file. Parents get proxy access to children **under 12** automatically; ages **12–17** need the **Teen Proxy Form**
   signed by the teen.
5. **Dunmore MyVisit app / patient portal invite.** Invite link is valid for **7 days**; activation needs date of
   birth + last 4 digits of the phone on file; support or the front desk can resend. **5 failed logins → locked
   for 30 minutes**; support can unlock sooner after the identity check. Supported on **iOS 16+ and Android 11+**.
   E-check-in opens **24 hours** before the appointment; clinics also have **DK-220 self-check-in kiosks**.
6. **No-show policy.** Cancel or reschedule at least **24 hours** ahead. Late cancel / no-show fee **$35**; the
   **first one in any 12 months is waived** as a courtesy. **3 no-shows in 12 months → MyVisit online booking paused
   for 6 months** (book by phone instead). Arriving **more than 15 minutes late** may require rescheduling.
   Waiver exceptions (emergencies, hospital stays) are reviewed by the Patient Support Lead.
7. **ClinicBoard scheduler (staff tool).** Visit lengths: New Patient 40 min, Annual Physical 40 min, Follow-up
   20 min, Virtual Visit 15 min. **Same-day sick slots released at 7:00 a.m.** daily. Waitlist offers openings by
   text and **holds the slot 30 minutes**. Visits needing prior authorization show **"Pending PA"** until approved.
8. **Prior authorization.** Dunmore's PA team submits within **2 business days** of the order. Insurer decisions
   typically **5–7 business days**; urgent requests **72 hours**. Status shows in MyVisit; patient gets a message when
   the decision arrives. If the patient changes insurance, PA must be resubmitted to the new plan.
9. **Dunmore Pay (billing).** Billing line 555-0148, Mon–Fri 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. CT; fax 555-0177. Statements go out
   on the **5th of each month**, due in **30 days**. **Prompt-pay discount: 10%** off a self-pay balance paid in full
   within 30 days of the first statement. **Payment plans:** balances of **$200 or more**, **interest-free**, up to
   **12 months**, minimum **$25/month** (Dunmore Pay > Payment Plans). Autopay runs on the **15th**. Guest Pay works
   with the statement account number + last name. One missed plan payment gets a reminder; two in a row cancels the plan.
10. **Disputes, EOBs, refunds.** Billing disputes acknowledged within **2 business days**, resolved within **10
    business days**; account placed on a **30-day billing hold** (no late notices). If an EOB shows a denial or a
    different amount, the patient uploads it in **Dunmore Pay > Upload EOB** (or faxes 555-0177); billing resubmits to
    the insurer within **5 business days**. Overpayments refunded to the original payment method within **14 business
    days**; credits **under $5** stay on the account unless the patient asks.
11. **Superbill.** MyVisit > Visits > Documents > "Request superbill"; ready within **2 business days**; includes
    visit codes, diagnosis codes and the provider's NPI, for out-of-network reimbursement.
12. **LabLink results.** Most results released within **3 business days**; pathology and imaging up to **7 business
    days** (released after provider review). Patients can message their provider in MyVisit (reply within **2 business
    days**) or request a care-team call-back.
13. **Self-pay prices.** Standard office visit **$95**, Virtual Visit **$45**, Annual Physical **$160**. Good-faith
    estimate sent within **1 business day** on request. Interpreter services are **free** (request when booking).
14. **Records.** Medical records via MyVisit > Records; electronic copy free, delivered within **10 business days**.
15. **Named leads.** **Marisol Okafor** — Patient Support Lead (no-show waivers, complaints; supervisor call-back
    within 1 business day). **Declan Whitcombe** — Billing Supervisor (disputes over $500, estate/deceased-patient
    accounts). **Noor Hadley** — Prior Authorization team lead.
