# Elmstead Credit — internal facts sheet (SYNTHETIC)

Elmstead Credit is a fictional member-owned credit union headquartered in Harrow Falls. Support runs on HelpLoom.
Style: formal, security-first. Every member-facing reply verifies identity before discussing any account detail.

1. **Identity verification ("3-point check")** before discussing any account: (a) last 4 digits of the member number,
   (b) date of birth, (c) the 6-digit one-time passcode (OTP) sent by text to the phone on file. Staff and chat NEVER ask
   for a full card number, PIN, online banking password or full member number. If the phone on file changed in the last
   30 days, the member must verify in person at a branch or through a VerifyLive video call.
2. **Member Service Center**: 555-0118, Mon–Fri 7:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. and Sat 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Central Time (CT),
   closed Sundays and federal holidays. **CardGuard 24/7 lost/stolen & fraud line**: 555-0142.
   Secure messages in Elmstead Online are answered within 8 business hours. Fraud call-backs within 2 hours during
   service hours.
3. **Branches**: Harrow Falls Main (HQ, 210 Orchard Ave), Linden Square, Quarry Road, Bellmere. Branch lobbies Mon–Fri
   9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. CT, Sat 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. CT (Bellmere closed Saturdays). Notary and medallion signatures
   at Harrow Falls Main only, by appointment.
4. **CardGuard card freeze**: Elmstead Online or app > Cards > CardGuard > Freeze. Takes effect immediately; unfreeze any
   time. A freeze blocks new purchases and ATM withdrawals but still allows refunds and existing recurring merchants.
   CardGuard Travel Mode replaces travel notices (set dates and countries).
5. **Replacement cards**: standard delivery 7–10 business days, free. Expedited delivery 2 business days for a $15 fee
   (waived for Summit Share Draft members). Lost/stolen cards are closed and reissued with a new number.
6. **Disputes** (Elmstead Online > Help > Dispute a Transaction, form DS-2): must be filed within 60 days of the
   statement date. Dispute provisional credit posts within 10 business days of a complete dispute. Investigation up to
   45 calendar days (90 days for foreign transactions or accounts open fewer than 30 days). Disputes team acknowledges
   within 1 business day.
7. **ACH holds and deposits**: an incoming ACH over $5,000 from a first-time originator gets a 2-business-day ACH hold.
   Mobile check deposit: limit $5,000 per day and $15,000 per rolling 30 days; cutoff 6:00 p.m. CT; first $225 available
   the next business day, remainder within 2 business days. External transfers in Elmstead Online: $10,000 per day.
   Elmstead QuickPay (person-to-person): $2,500 per day.
8. **Share drafts** (checking): Everyday Share Draft — no monthly fee. Summit Share Draft — $8/month, waived with a
   $2,500 average daily balance or $1,000+ monthly direct deposit; includes free expedited cards and 4 out-of-network ATM
   fee refunds per month. Primary Share (savings) holds the $5 membership share, which must stay on deposit.
   Stop payment on a share draft: $30. Courtesy Pay overdraft fee: $28, maximum 3 per day. Overdraft Transfer from
   savings: $5.
9. **Round-Up Savings**: rounds each debit card purchase up to the next dollar and sweeps the round-ups from the share
   draft to the Primary Share nightly. First-year 10% match, capped at $100, paid on the account anniversary. Pause or
   resume in Elmstead Online > Savings > Round-Up.
10. **Elmstead Auto Loans**: new vehicles from 5.49% APR up to 72 months; used vehicles (model year 2019 or newer) from
    6.19% APR up to 66 months. 10-day payment grace period, then a $25 late fee. Due date may be changed once every 12
    months. Payoff quotes are valid for 10 days. Lien release within 10 business days of payoff (electronic title
    states); paper titles mailed within 15 business days. GAP coverage: one-time $395.
11. **Elmstead Online sign-in lockout**: 5 failed sign-ins lock the account for 30 minutes. A second lockout within
    24 hours requires a call to the Member Service Center for a reset (after the 3-point check).
12. **Wires**: outgoing domestic wire fee $20, cutoff 2:00 p.m. CT; requires a call-back verification for first-time
    recipients.
13. **Support leads**: Renata Ostrowski (Member Service Center manager), Desmond Achterberg (Card Services lead),
    Priya Valcourt (Lending support lead), Tobias Wren (Fraud & Disputes lead).
14. **Escalation tiers**: Tier 1 = Member Service Center; Tier 2 = Card Services / Lending; Tier 3 = Fraud & Disputes.
    Suspected account takeover goes straight to Tier 3 and triggers an immediate card freeze and Elmstead Online
    credential reset.
