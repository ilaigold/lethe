# Granite Insurance — internal facts sheet (SYNTHETIC)

Granite Insurance is a fictional auto and home insurance carrier headquartered in Cedar Bluff. Support runs on HelpLoom.
Products: **Granite Mobile** (policyholder app), **ClaimTrack** (claim portal, web + inside Granite Mobile),
**SafeMiles telematics** (usage-based auto discount), **HomeShield policy** (homeowners).
Style: structured. Every reply gives three labeled parts: **Claim status** (or **Policy status** for non-claim
questions), **Next step**, and **Who will contact you** (a named team or person and a deadline).

1. **Contact & hours**: 24/7 Claims Line **555-0186** (first notice of loss, roadside, emergency mitigation dispatch).
   Customer Care (policy service, billing, SafeMiles): **555-0129**, Mon–Fri 7:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. and Sat 8:00 a.m.–2:00
   p.m. Mountain Time (MT), closed Sundays. Messages sent in ClaimTrack or Granite Mobile are answered within
   **4 business hours**. Granite never asks for a full SSN, a bank login, or payment by gift card through text or email.
2. **First notice of loss (FNOL)**: report in Granite Mobile (Claims > Report a Claim), on ClaimTrack, or on the Claims
   Line. A claim number in the format **GC-** + 6 digits (e.g. GC-204118) is issued immediately. The assigned adjuster
   makes first contact within **24 hours for auto** claims and **48 hours for HomeShield** claims (**72 hours** during a
   declared catastrophe / CAT event). Theft claims need a police report number at FNOL.
3. **ClaimTrack stages**: Reported → Assigned → Inspection → Estimate → Settlement → Paid → Closed. Customers upload
   photos, receipts and documents in ClaimTrack; every stage change pushes a notification to Granite Mobile. Third
   parties (contractors, body shops, family members) are authorized in ClaimTrack > Claim > Contacts.
4. **Auto inspections**: **PhotoEstimate** in Granite Mobile needs 8 photos (4 corners of the vehicle, 2 damage
   close-ups, VIN plate, odometer); the estimate is posted within **2 business days** of upload. Or drive in, no
   appointment needed, to a Drive-In Claim Center: **Cedar Bluff** (400 Quarry Lane, HQ), **Ridgeview**, **Lowell Flats**,
   open Mon–Fri 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. MT. Non-drivable vehicles get an adjuster visit at the tow yard or shop within
   **3 business days**. Body shop supplements submitted through ClaimTrack are reviewed within **2 business days**.
5. **HomeShield adjuster visit**: a field adjuster visit is scheduled within **3 business days** of FNOL
   (**5 business days** during a CAT event). Emergency mitigation (tarping, water extraction, board-up) is reimbursed up
   to **$2,500 without pre-approval** with receipts; Granite Rapid Response vendors dispatch within **4 hours** through
   the Claims Line.
6. **Deductibles** (shown on the declarations page): auto comprehensive and collision options $250 / $500 / $1,000.
   Windshield chip repair is **$0 deductible** on every auto tier; windshield replacement uses the comprehensive
   deductible (except Premier). HomeShield all-perils options $500 / $1,000 / $2,500 (default $1,000); **wind/hail
   deductible = 2% of Coverage A** (dwelling). Deductible changes take effect the next day and never apply to open claims.
7. **Auto tiers**: **Granite Auto Basic** (no roadside, no rental; rental endorsement $4/month = $30/day up to 30 days);
   **Granite Auto Plus** (roadside with 15-mile tow, rental $40/day up to 30 days); **Granite Auto Premier** (50-mile tow,
   rental $60/day up to 30 days, $0 windshield replacement, accident forgiveness on the first at-fault accident).
   Roadside requests: Granite Mobile > Roadside or the Claims Line.
8. **HomeShield tiers**: **HomeShield Essential** (contents at actual cash value, additional living expenses (ALE) up to
   6 months, no water backup; water backup endorsement $6/month for $5,000); **HomeShield Plus** (contents at replacement
   cost, ALE up to 12 months, water backup $10,000 included). A sudden burst pipe is covered under all-perils on both
   tiers; sewer/drain backup needs water backup coverage.
9. **SafeMiles telematics**: enroll in Granite Mobile > SafeMiles. The **SafeMiles Tag SM-200** (Bluetooth windshield tag)
   ships within **5 business days**. 5% enrollment discount right away; 90-day scoring period; SafeMiles score 0–100.
   Renewal discount: score 90+ = 25%, 80–89 = 15%, 70–79 = 10%, below 70 keeps the 5%. SafeMiles **never raises** a
   premium. Trips can be marked "I wasn't driving" within **7 days**. Tag replacement free once per policy term, then $15.
   Program lead: Daria Lindqvist.
10. **Billing**: pay-in-full discount 6%. Monthly installments carry a $5 installment fee, waived with autopay (EFT).
    10-day grace period after the due date; a $10 late fee applies only after the grace period. A cancellation notice
    gives 20 days to pay; reinstatement with no lapse within 30 days of cancellation if paid in full with a signed
    no-loss statement. Billing disputes reviewed within 1 business day; refunds post in 3–5 business days. Policy
    cancellation has no fee; the pro rata refund is issued within 10 business days.
11. **Claim payments**: issued within **5 business days** of a signed settlement. Direct deposit through ClaimTrack lands in
    1–2 business days after issue; mailed checks take 7–10 business days. HomeShield dwelling payments over $10,000
    list the mortgage company as co-payee.
12. **Total loss & theft**: a vehicle is a total loss when the repair estimate exceeds **75% of actual cash value (ACV)**.
    Total loss valuation within 5 business days of inspection; rental continues 5 days after the total loss offer.
    A stolen vehicle not recovered within 14 days is settled as a total loss.
13. **Subrogation**: when another driver is at fault, Granite pursues subrogation and refunds the deductible (in
    proportion to the amount recovered), typically within 60–120 days.
14. **Policy documents & changes**: declarations page and ID cards in Granite Mobile > Policy > Documents. A newly acquired
    vehicle is covered automatically for 14 days if reported within 14 days. Lender / mortgagee changes are processed
    within 2 business days and evidence of insurance is sent to the lender directly. Granite Mobile locks for 15
    minutes after 5 failed sign-ins.
15. **Support leads & escalation**: Marisol Quenneville (Claims Contact Center manager), Owen Thackeray (Auto Claims lead),
    Beatrix Holloway (HomeShield Property Claims lead), Felix Arundel (Policy Service & Billing lead), Daria Lindqvist
    (SafeMiles program lead). Tier 1 = Customer Care / claims intake; Tier 2 = adjuster team lead (auto: Owen Thackeray,
    property: Beatrix Holloway); Tier 3 = **Claims Review Desk** (coverage disputes and complaints), responds within
    **2 business days**.
