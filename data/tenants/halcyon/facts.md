# Halcyon Travel — internal support facts sheet (SYNTHETIC)

Fictional online travel agency (flights, hotels, packages). All names, prices, airlines, hotels, booking references and
accounts are invented. Every record in `raw.jsonl` must stay consistent with this sheet.

Reply style: calm and solution-first. Lead with the fix, give **two options with prices or credits**, then ask which
the traveler prefers.

## 1. Support hours, channels and leads
- **Traveler Care desk**: daily 07:00–23:00 **Eastern Time (ET)**, run from the **Harborview care center**.
  Email halcyon.care@example.com, phone 555-0163, in-app chat (Halcyon Trips app > Help > Chat).
- **Urgent Travel line**: 24/7 at **555-0168**, for travelers departing within **72 hours** or already traveling
  (stranded, missed connection, denied boarding). Overnight (23:00–07:00 ET) is staffed by the **Westgate care center**.
- Leads: **Mireille Fontaine** (Head of Traveler Care), **Desmond Achterberg** (Schedule Change desk lead),
  **Rosalind Pike** (Refunds & Credits lead), **Tomasz Vrba** (Halcyon Protect claims liaison),
  **Nadia Okafor-Hale** (Halcyon Club / Concierge team lead).

## 2. SLAs (first response)
- **Urgent** (departure within 72 hours, or in-trip): **1 hour**, 24/7. Concierge members: **15 minutes**.
- **Standard**: **12 hours**. Voyager members: **6 hours**.
- **Refund and claim tickets**: **3 business days**.
- Refunds reach the original payment method in **7–10 business days**. Travel credit is issued within **1 hour** and
  shows in the app's **Trip Wallet**.

## 3. Reference formats
- Booking reference: **HT-** + 6 letters/digits (e.g. HT-4QX7LM). Always confirm it with the lead traveler's surname.
- Halcyon Club account: **HCA-####**. Travel credit: **TC-####**. Halcyon Protect claim: **PC-####** (4 digits each).

## 4. 24-hour hold and 24-hour cancellation
- **24-hour hold**: any flight-only booking can be held at the quoted fare for **24 hours, free** ("Hold this fare" in
  the app, or by an agent). Max **3 active holds per account**. Not extendable; when it lapses, seats are released and
  the fare is re-quoted. Packages and StayPlus hotels cannot be held.
- Free cancellation for a **full refund within 24 hours of purchase**, if departure is at least **7 days** away.

## 5. FlexFare (add-on, per traveler per trip; add at booking or within 24 hours of booking)
- **FlexFare Basic — $29**: **one** date/time change, change fee waived, traveler pays any fare difference.
- **FlexFare Plus — $59**: **unlimited** changes up to **3 hours before departure**, change fee waived; if the new fare
  is lower, the difference comes back as travel credit; cancel up to 3 hours before departure for **100% travel credit**.
- **No FlexFare**: **$75 change fee per traveler** + fare difference. Cancelling a non-refundable fare gives travel
  credit of the fare **minus $75 per traveler**.

## 6. Schedule changes (airline-initiated)
- Change of **2 hours or more** (departure or arrival), a new connection, or a different airport: traveler chooses
  (a) free rebooking on another flight of the same airline within **±3 days**, no fare difference; (b) **full refund**
  to the original payment; or (c) **travel credit worth 110%** of the ticket value.
- Change **under 2 hours**: accept it, or move flights paying the fare difference only (change fee waived as a
  schedule-change courtesy).
- The traveler must respond within **7 days** of the notice (app: Trips > Review change); after that the new schedule
  is auto-accepted. Schedule Change desk lead: Desmond Achterberg.

## 7. Travel credit
- Valid **12 months** from issue. Usable on flights, StayPlus hotels and packages; **not** on Halcyon Protect or FlexFare.
- Only for travelers on the same Halcyon account; max **3 credits per booking**.
- One-time **6-month extension**, only if requested **before** expiry; approved by Refunds & Credits (Rosalind Pike).
- Goodwill credit limits: agent up to **$150 per booking**; up to **$400** with a lead's approval; above $400 needs
  Mireille Fontaine.

## 8. StayPlus hotels
- **StayPlus Flexible** rate (pay at hotel): free cancellation until **48 hours before check-in** (15:00 hotel local time).
  Cancelled later than that, or a no-show: the hotel charges the **first night**.
- **StayPlus Saver** rate (prepaid, about 15% cheaper): non-refundable; cancelled more than **7 days** before check-in →
  travel credit minus **one night**; within 7 days → no credit.
- Partner hotels used in examples: Harbor Lantern Hotel (Lisbon), The Calloway Suites (Denver), Marisol Bay Resort
  (Cancún), Alder & Pine Lodge (Banff). Special requests (late checkout, connecting rooms) are passed on, never guaranteed.

## 9. Halcyon Protect insurance (underwritten by the fictional Wexmoor Assurance)
- **Protect Essential — 5% of trip cost**: cancellation for covered reasons (illness, injury, jury duty, severe weather),
  trip delay **$150/day after 6 hours** (max $600), baggage up to **$1,000**. Can be bought any time before departure.
- **Protect Plus — 8% of trip cost**: everything in Essential plus **Cancel For Any Reason** (**75% cash** back if
  cancelled at least **48 hours** before departure), trip delay $250/day (max $1,000), baggage up to **$2,500**.
  Must be bought within **14 days** of the first booking payment.
- **10-day free look**: full premium refund if cancelled within 10 days of purchase and before travel.
- Claims: app > Trips > Protect > File claim, within **30 days after the trip ends**; claim number PC-####;
  decision within **10 business days** of complete documents. Liaison: Tomasz Vrba.

## 10. Packages (flight + StayPlus hotel)
- Deposit **$200 per traveler**; balance auto-charged **45 days before departure**.
- Cancel **45+ days** out: deposit kept as travel credit, rest refunded. **15–44 days**: fee of **50%** of the package
  price. **Under 15 days**: non-refundable (Halcyon Protect may reimburse).

## 11. Halcyon Connect (self-transfer itineraries sold by Halcyon)
- If a delay on the first flight makes the traveler miss a Halcyon Connect connection, Halcyon rebooks the next
  available flight for free or refunds the unused segment, and covers a hotel up to **$180/night** if stuck overnight.
  Traveler must call the Urgent Travel line before buying anything themselves.

## 12. Halcyon Club membership tiers
- **Explorer — free**: standard SLAs.
- **Voyager — $79/year**: FlexFare Basic included on every flight booking; 6-hour standard SLA.
- **Concierge — $249/year**: FlexFare Plus included; dedicated care agent; 15-minute urgent SLA; 2 airport lounge
  passes per year.

## 13. Halcyon Trips app
- Current version **6.4** (September 2026). **Trip Wallet** shows travel credits; boarding passes are saved offline.
- Known bug in **6.3**: Trip Wallet shows a **$0.00** balance after login. Credits are not lost; updating to 6.4 fixes it.
- Schedule change alerts open the **Review change** screen with the options from section 6.

## 14. Name corrections
- Typos of up to **3 characters** are corrected free (agent files it with the airline). Full name changes or transfers
  to another person are not allowed: cancel and rebook under the normal rules.
